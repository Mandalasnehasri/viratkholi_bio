Career

Virat Kohli is one of the most iconic cricketers in the world and a modern-day legend of the game. He made his international debut in August 2008 in an ODI match against Sri Lanka and quickly rose through the ranks with his aggressive batting style and consistent performances. Over the years, he established himself as a mainstay in the Indian batting lineup and eventually took over as captain in all three formats. Kohli has broken numerous records, including becoming the fastest player to reach 8,000, 9,000, 10,000, and even 12,000 ODI runs. Under his leadership, India achieved significant victories, and he played a vital role in elevating the fitness and performance standards of the team. In the Indian Premier League (IPL), Kohli has been a constant presence for Royal Challengers Bangalore (RCB) and served as their captain for several seasons.

Educational Life

Virat Kohli grew up in Delhi and studied at Vishal Bharti Public School, where he was first introduced to cricket. His natural talent was evident from a young age, and he later moved to Saviour Convent School in Paschim Vihar to focus more on sports. At just nine years old, he joined the West Delhi Cricket Academy (WDCA), where he trained under coach Rajkumar Sharma. While Kohli was good in academics, his passion for cricket soon took precedence, and he dedicated most of his time and energy to the sport, laying the foundation for what would become an extraordinary career.

Marriage Life

Virat Kohli married renowned Bollywood actress Anushka Sharma on December 11, 2017, in a beautiful and intimate ceremony held in Tuscany, Italy. The couple has been admired for their grace, simplicity, and strong partnership both in personal and professional life. Together, they have become one of India’s most beloved celebrity couples, often making headlines for their philanthropic work, public appearances, and mutual support in their respective careers.
