About

Virat Kohli (born November 5, 1988) is an Indian international cricketer and former captain of the Indian national team. Widely regarded as one of the greatest batsmen of all time, he is known for his passion, consistency, and unmatched dedication to the sport. Kohli has set numerous records across all formats of cricket.Virat Kohli is one of the greatest cricketers of all time, known for his aggressive batting and passionate leadership

Career

Virat Kohli is one of the most iconic cricketers in the world and a modern-day legend of the game. He made his international debut in August 2008 in an ODI match against Sri Lanka and quickly rose through the ranks with his aggressive batting style and consistent performances. Over the years, he established himself as a mainstay in the Indian batting lineup and eventually took over as captain in all three formats. Kohli has broken numerous records, including becoming the fastest player to reach 8,000, 9,000, 10,000, and even 12,000 ODI runs. Under his leadership, India achieved significant victories, and he played a vital role in elevating the fitness and performance standards of the team. In the Indian Premier League (IPL), Kohli has been a constant presence for Royal Challengers Bangalore (RCB) and served as their captain for several seasons.

Educational Life

Virat Kohli grew up in Delhi and studied at Vishal Bharti Public School, where he was first introduced to cricket.
